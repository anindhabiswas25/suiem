# Suiem Privacy Notice: DRAFT 2026-11-10.1

> **Draft pending legal review (OQ-L-03, OQ-L-07).**

- We collect only what we need: your email (login), passkey public keys, preferences you choose, consent records and, if you link one, a public wallet address.
- We never collect private keys, seed phrases or raw browsing history. Nothing personal is written onchain.
- Logs are redacted. Emails, addresses, codes and tokens are not logged.
- You can see your data inventory, export it and request deletion in Settings.
- Third parties: email delivery (local Mailpit in development). No advertising, no data sale.
